All four of these are doable after work in June without a headlamp. Sorted best
to worst on 5:30pm parking.

1. **Van Bibber Park** - flat, fast, dog-heavy, parking almost always fine.
2. **Two Ponds** - small, quiet, and genuinely full of birds.
3. **Majestic View** - short, exposed, best in the last hour of light.
4. **Ralston Creek** - the nicest of the four and the one you will circle for.
