Draw a line down Federal Boulevard and then along I-25. West of it — Arvada,
the Jeffco part of Westminster — is an appreciation market that behaves like
Denver's northwest side. East of it — Northglenn, Thornton, Federal Heights,
the Adams part of Westminster — is a yield market with a heavier tax bill.
The Adams County median was $520,000 in June 2026; Arvada sits comfortably
above it and Northglenn comfortably below.

## Arvada: appreciation

[Olde Town Arvada](/neighborhoods/arvada/olde-town-arvada) and the G Line
(2019) turned Arvada from a bedroom suburb into the northwest's second
centre. The stock:

| Area | Stock | 3-bed, 2026 | Rent | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Olde Town and the 1900s–40s grid | Bungalows, cottages | $600–780K | $3,000–3,400 | G Line station; walkable; yields near 5% |
| East Arvada (Sheridan to Wadsworth, 52nd–72nd) | 1950s–60s ranches | $530–620K | $2,850–3,100 | The Arvada version of the Denver ring; Ralston Creek trail |
| West Arvada (Kipling to Indiana) | 1970s–90s | $580–700K | $3,000–3,300 | Jeffco's Ralston Valley schools; Apex rec center |
| Candelas, Leyden Rock, Whisper Creek | 2005–2026 master-planned | $650–850K | $3,300–3,800 | Metro districts 40–70 mills; HOAs; Rocky Flats adjacency still a disclosure |

Arvada has no long-term rental licence; its 2026 STR update tightened the
number of permits per person. Mill levies around 90–100; higher in the
metro-district communities.

## Westminster: the line runs through it

West of Federal (Jeffco): 1960s–80s on the Standley Lake side, Jeffco
schools, priced $560,000–680,000 and renting $2,900–3,200 —
[Standley Lake](/neighborhoods/westminster/standley-lake) is its own
sub-market of 1970s–90s houses with lake and mountain views. East of
Federal (Adams): 1950s–70s ranches in old Westminster and Shaw Heights,
Westminster Public Schools, $460,000–540,000 and renting $2,600–2,850. The
Westminster Station area on the B Line (71st and Federal) has new
apartments and a 2020s "downtown" project that stalled and restarted.
Westminster allows one STR per licensee.

## Northglenn, Thornton, Federal Heights: yield

| City | Stock | 3-bed, 2026 | Rent | Gross yield | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Northglenn](/places/northglenn) | 1960s–70s ranches and split-levels, a planned community of its era | $440–510K | $2,550–2,800 | 6.6% | Non-primary STRs licensed; N Line at 112th; Adams 12 |
| [Thornton](/places/thornton) south of 120th | 1960s–90s | $450–540K | $2,600–2,900 | 6.4% | N Line stations at 88th and 104th; Adams 12; the Original Thornton blocks are cheapest |
| Thornton north of 120th | 1995–2026 subdivisions | $540–680K | $2,950–3,400 | 6.0% | Metro districts; 27J schools east of Colorado Blvd (four-day week); Heritage Todd Creek |
| [Federal Heights](/places/federal-heights) | 1960s–70s small houses; Water World | $400–460K | $2,400–2,650 | 7.0% | The cheapest houses in the metro north of Sheridan; thin resale; tight tenant pool |

Adams 12 Five Star levies are the reason these tax bills run 30–50% above
Denver's for the same value; see [the property-tax guide](/investing/property-tax-for-colorado-investors).
None of the four licenses long-term rentals; Northglenn is the north
metro's one non-primary STR jurisdiction. See [the north metro rules](/investing/north-metro-rental-rules).

The Northglenn house costs $320 a month less to hold and appreciated twenty
points less over the last decade. That is the whole trade.

## What is specific to the north metro

- **Two rail lines.** The G Line (Arvada, Wheat Ridge) and the N Line
  (Northglenn, Thornton). Station premiums are small on houses, real on
  tenant demand.
- **Expansive soils** in Thornton and Northglenn east of I-25; the
  Denver-Julesburg formation's clays are the reason for the
  structural-engineer clause in so many Adams County inspection reports.
- **27J's four-day school week** east of Colorado Boulevard in Thornton and
  in Brighton; families ask.
- **Oil and gas.** Thornton and north Westminster have active well pads and
  the state's 2,000-foot setback rule (2020); check the COGCC map for
  anything north of 136th.

## Where we would look first

East Arvada's 1950s–60s ranches between Sheridan and Wadsworth for the
balanced buy — Arvada's appreciation at the lowest Arvada entry price.
Northglenn's Hillcrest and Croke Reservoir blocks for yield with an N Line
station and an STR option. Federal Heights only as the fourth or fifth
property, with a manager who knows it.
