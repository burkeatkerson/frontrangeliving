These are not the same asset class wearing different zip codes, and treating
them as comparable is how out-of-state buyers get hurt here.

## Longmont

Employment base is real and diversified, resale liquidity is deep, and the
gross yield is not going to excite anyone. You are buying a ten-year hold in a
market that has never had a bad decade.

## Pueblo

The yield is genuinely high and the reason is genuinely visible: thinner
demand, older stock, and a resale market that can go quiet for a quarter. If
your mandate is cash flow and you can tolerate a longer exit, the math works.

## Where we would actually put money

Longmont, if this is your only Colorado asset. Pueblo, if it is your fourth and
the portfolio needs yield.
