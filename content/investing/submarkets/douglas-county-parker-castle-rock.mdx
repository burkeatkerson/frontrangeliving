Douglas County is 380,000 people, a median household income above $130,000,
the Douglas County School District, and housing that is overwhelmingly
post-1985 master-planned. Tenants with children choose it for the schools
and the safety statistics and pay for both. Landlords pay for the metro
districts. The [property-tax guide](/investing/property-tax-for-colorado-investors)
explains the mechanism; this brief is about where in the county it bites
least.

## What is specific to Douglas County

- **Douglas County School District** is the tenant draw, county-wide. Its
  levy is moderate; it is the metro districts that push total mills up.
- **The HOA layer.** Nearly every parcel; dues of $50–150 a month on a
  house, $250–450 on a townhome, with minimum-lease covenants almost
  everywhere and rental caps in some post-2010 communities.
- **No STRs.** Parker prohibits them; Castle Rock's covenants and Highlands
  Ranch's PD rules and HRCA exclude them in practice. See [the Douglas County
  rules](/investing/douglas-county-rental-rules). This is a long-term,
  family-tenant county.
- **No rental licence** anywhere in the county.
- **Wildfire** on the west (Roxborough, Perry Park, Castle Pines's west
  edge, the Pinery's ponderosa); **hail** on the east plain.
- **Water.** Douglas County is largely on deep aquifer wells through its
  water districts, and the districts' renewable-water projects (WISE,
  Rueter-Hess) are the reason tap fees and district levies are high.
  Xeriscape rules are strict in Castle Rock.
- **I-25 and Parker Road.** The county's two commute spines; Parker Road's
  congestion lowers rents in the far south-east by $100–200.

## Where we would look first

A 1995–2005 four-bed in Stonegate, Canterberry or Founders Village, whose
district debt has been retired or refinanced down, with a tenant who chose
the school. Skip Highlands Ranch and Lone Tree as rentals. Buy south of
Hess or in Crystal Valley only from builder standing inventory, having read
the service plan, and with a plan to hold through the district's levy.
