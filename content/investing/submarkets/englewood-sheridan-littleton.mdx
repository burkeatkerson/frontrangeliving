South of Yale Avenue the Denver grid continues into three cities that
Denver's buyers often skip and Denver's tenants do not. Englewood and
Sheridan are the 1950s bungalow belt of [Platt Park](/neighborhoods/denver/platt-park)
and [Rosedale](/neighborhoods/denver/rosedale), continued south across the
county line at a $100,000 discount. Littleton is older, bigger, and has a
downtown.

## Englewood

Seven square miles, 35,000 people, and two things that make it a rental
market: the hospital cluster — Swedish Medical Center, Craig Hospital,
Porter Adventist a mile north, roughly 8,000 jobs in a few blocks around
Hampden and Logan — and the D Line, with stations at Englewood (CityCenter)
and Oxford.

| Area | Stock | 3-bed, 2026 | Rent | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| North Englewood (Yale to Hampden, Broadway to University) | 1940s–50s bungalows | $500–600K | $2,800–3,050 | Closest to Swedish/Craig; Rosedale spillover pricing; the mid-term furnished core |
| CityCenter and the D Line blocks | 1950s ranches; 2000s townhomes at the station | $470–560K | $2,650–2,900 | Englewood's civic center on the old Cinderella City site; station walkability |
| South Englewood (Belleview corridor) | 1950s–60s | $480–560K | $2,700–2,900 | Bigger lots; Cherry Hills Village adjacent to the east |
| Broadway and Santa Fe frontage | Small multi-units, mixed commercial | $600–900K (2–4 units) | $3,800–5,000 | The south metro's small-multifamily stock; noise and traffic |

Gross yields of 6–6.4%. Mill levies around 95–105 (Englewood Schools).
Englewood's 2023 STR ordinance: primary residence, 185 days, two per bedroom
up to eight, in a primary dwelling or ADU — see [the south metro rules](/investing/south-metro-rental-rules).
The 30-day furnished rental to hospital staff is the investor's furnished
play here and a strong one; see [the mid-term rental guide](/investing/mid-term-rentals-hospital-corridors).

## Sheridan

Three square miles between Englewood and the Platte, 6,000 people, 1950s–60s
small houses and the River Point retail center. Three-beds at $420,000–480,000
renting $2,450–2,700 — gross yields near 7%, the south metro's best. Sheridan
Schools are small and not a draw; the tenant pool is working households who
want a house with a yard under $2,700 within fifteen minutes of downtown. The
resale market is thin: a few dozen houses a year. Buy it as the fourth
property, not the first, and with a manager who knows Federal Boulevard.

## Littleton

Thirteen square miles, 45,000 people, and a real downtown —
[Downtown Littleton](/neighborhoods/littleton/downtown-littleton), Main
Street, the 1890s grid, and the Littleton/Downtown station on the C and D
Lines. Littleton Public Schools are a tenant draw across the whole city.

| Area | Stock | 3-bed, 2026 | Rent | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Downtown and the 1890s–1920s grid | Victorian and bungalow | $650–900K | $3,200–3,800 | Premium; yields under 5.5%; Littleton's STR licence parking rule bites here |
| 1950s–60s Littleton (Broadway to Santa Fe, Littleton Blvd to Belleview) | Ranches | $530–620K | $2,900–3,150 | The balanced buy; LPS schools; Arapahoe Community College |
| Ketring Park, Bow Mar edge | 1960s–70s larger houses | $650–850K | $3,300–3,700 | Priced off Bow Mar; yields under 5.5% |
| Southbridge, Columbine edges (unincorporated Jeffco/Arapahoe) | 1970s–80s | $560–660K | $3,000–3,300 | County, not city, rules; check jurisdiction on the parcel |

Littleton's STR licence: annual, two per bedroom up to eight, 28-night
maximum stay, one parking space per bedroom, two-hour local contact. No
long-term licence.

Furnished at $3,300 for 30–90-day hospital stays, self-managed, the gap

## What is specific to the south side

- **Santa Fe Drive.** Four lanes of the metro's worst commute, and the
  houses that back onto it rent for $200 less. The D Line is the reason the
  blocks east of it rent better.
- **The Platte River floodplain.** Sheridan and west Englewood have parcels
  in it; flood insurance is a line item on those.
- **Englewood's 1950s sewer and water laterals** are the same clay as
  Denver's; scope them.
- **Cherry Hills Village and Bow Mar** adjacency lifts the east and south
  edges of both Englewood and Littleton.

## Where we would look first

North Englewood between Broadway and University for the balanced buy with a
furnished-rental option. The 1950s Littleton ranches between Broadway and
Santa Fe for the family-tenant market under Littleton Public Schools.
Sheridan for yield, later.
