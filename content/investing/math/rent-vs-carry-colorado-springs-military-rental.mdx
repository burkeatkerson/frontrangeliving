Colorado Springs has roughly 40,000 active-duty personnel across Fort Carson,
Peterson and Schriever Space Force Bases, the Air Force Academy and Cheyenne
Mountain, and the Army pays most of them a housing allowance that is public
information. That makes the south and east sides of the city one of the few
places on the Front Range where you can underwrite rent from a government
table rather than a listing site.

## The assumptions

- **Purchase:** $400,000, a 1990s–2000s three-bed, two-bath, two-car house in
  the Security-Widefield / Fountain / southeast Springs band, under the
  Colorado Springs single-family median of $499,900 (CAR, June 2026).
- **Financing:** 25% down, $300,000 loan at 7.25%.
- **Rent:** $2,200. Leases on comparable houses this summer ran $2,100–2,300;
  the E-5 with-dependents BAH of $2,358 is the ceiling most of the tenant pool
  prices against.
- **Tenant mix:** roughly half military, half civilian defence contractors and
  hospital staff.

| **Total carry** | **$3,052** |
| **Rent** | **$2,200** |
| **Monthly gap** | **−$852** |

With self-management the gap is about −$650; we quote −$750 as the realistic
middle. It is a smaller gap than Denver's on a smaller purchase, and the
gross yield of 6.6% is a full two points better.

## Why the rent is sticky here

BAH resets every January from a survey of local rents, and it has risen in the
Colorado Springs housing area in each of the last five years. A tenant paying
with BAH is not price-sensitive below the allowance; they are sensitive to
proximity to the gate, a garage, a fenced yard, and a school. Houses that
have all four rent in days. Houses without a garage sit.

## The two risks specific to this tenant base

**Orders.** Under the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act a tenant with PCS or
deployment orders can break the lease on 30 days' notice. It is not a
negotiation. Underwrite an extra turnover every third year, about $1,500 in
make-ready and a month of vacancy.

**Concentration.** The southeast Springs economy is the Department of
Defense. A base realignment is unlikely but not impossible, and a government
shutdown delays BAH payments, which most tenants bridge but some cannot. Keep
four months of carry in reserve rather than the two you would hold in Denver.

## Where to look, specifically

- **Security-Widefield and Fountain** (see the [Fountain / Security-Widefield
  brief](/investing/fountain-security-widefield)): the 1970s–2000s stock
  closest to Gate 20, $330,000–420,000.
- **Southeast Colorado Springs** along the Academy and Hancock corridors:
  older, cheaper, higher vacancy, more civilian.
- **Stetson Hills and the Powers corridor**: 1990s–2010s, $420,000–520,000,
  rents $2,300–2,600, nearer Peterson and Schriever than Carson. Look for
  metro district mills on anything built after 2005.

## What would make us walk

An STR pro forma. Non-owner-occupied short-term rentals are barred in the
city's single-family zones and 500-foot separated elsewhere — see [the
Springs STR page](/investing/short-term-rental-colorado-springs). This is a
long-term rental market, and a good one, and it does not need the Airbnb
story to work.
