Short answer: yes, with a licence. The part that changed, and the part that
kills most out-of-state pro formas, is the non-owner-occupied track.

> This is a summary of a municipal ordinance, current as of the date above. It
> is not legal advice, and ordinances on this topic change faster than almost
> anything else we track. Verify with the city before you write an offer.

## Owner-occupied

Still straightforward. Annual permit, life-safety inspection, proof of primary
residence.

## Non-owner-occupied

Density-capped by zone with a 500-foot separation from the next non-owner-occupied
permit. In the neighborhoods investors actually want, the caps are already met,
so the practical question is whether an existing permit conveys - and whether the
seller can prove it is in good standing.
