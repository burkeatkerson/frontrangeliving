Short answer: yes, with a permit. The part that changed, and the part that
kills most out-of-state pro formas, is the non-owner-occupied track.

<Note label="Read this first">
  This is a summary of a municipal ordinance, current as of the revision
  date above. It is not legal advice, and ordinances on this topic change
  faster than almost anything else we track. Verify with the city before you
  write an offer.
</Note>

## Owner-occupied

Still straightforward. Annual permit, life-safety self-certification, proof
that the unit is the operator's primary residence — occupied at least 185
days a year. The city launched a primary-residency audit programme in
February 2025 and has revoked permits on the strength of it. Renting a room,
a basement, or the whole house while you travel is all within the permit.

## Non-owner-occupied

- **Prohibited outright** in the single-family zones: R-E, R-1 6, R-1 9 and
  residential PDZs. That is most of the north and west side, Briargate, the
  Broadmoor, Rockrimmon and Mountain Shadows.
- **500-foot separation** from any other non-owner-occupied permit in the
  zones where it is allowed (R-2, R-4, R-5, mixed-use and commercial).
- Occupancy of two per bedroom plus two, maximum fifteen; weekly trash
  service; off-street parking; local contact; lodgers' and sales tax.

In the neighborhoods investors actually want — [Old Colorado
City](/neighborhoods/colorado-springs/old-colorado-city), the [Old North
End](/neighborhoods/colorado-springs/old-north-end), the blocks around
[Downtown](/neighborhoods/colorado-springs/downtown-colorado-springs) and
Manitou Avenue — the 500-foot spacing is already spoken for, so the practical
question is whether an existing permit conveys and whether the seller can
prove it is in good standing. The city publishes a map of active permits;
check it before you assume a parcel qualifies.

## The 2025 change: ADUs

Ordinance 25-45, Colorado Springs's implementation of the state ADU law,
severed STRs from ADUs. From June 30, 2025 a property with an ADU cannot
receive an STR permit. Properties that held both legally before that date
continue as non-conforming uses, which do not survive a lapse. Buyers who
planned to live in the house and nightly-rent the carriage house — the
classic Westside play — should confirm the property's status before closing.

## What has not changed

Long-term rentals in Colorado Springs are not licensed or registered. The
mid-term furnished rental — 30 days or more, to UCHealth Memorial and
Penrose staff, TDY military and relocating families — is a long-term rental
under the code and is the lawful route for an investor who wants furnished
income. See [the mid-term rental guide](/investing/mid-term-rentals-hospital-corridors)
and [the Springs submarket brief](/investing/colorado-springs-submarkets).
