The south metro's rental stock splits in two: the 1950s bungalows and small
multi-units of [Englewood](/places/englewood), [Sheridan](/places/sheridan)
and old [Littleton](/places/littleton) along Broadway and Santa Fe, and the
HOA-governed subdivisions of [Centennial](/places/centennial) and
[Greenwood Village](/places/greenwood-village) to the east. The first group
is where the investors are; the second is where the covenants are.

## Long-term rentals

No Arapahoe County city licenses or registers long-term rentals. State law
governs; see [the statewide page](/investing/colorado-landlord-law-2026).

## Englewood

Englewood's STR ordinance (2023) is a primary-residence rule with
specifics:

- Permitted in zones R-1-A, R-1-B, R-1-C, R-2-A, R-2-B, MU-R-3-A, -B and -C.
- The unit — a primary dwelling **or an ADU on the same lot** — must be the
  primary residence of one or more owners, who must live there **at least
  185 days a year**.
- Occupancy: two adults per bedroom, **maximum eight**.
- City sales tax (3.8%) and lodger's tax (2%) on every stay; an STR licence
  and business licence before advertising.

Investor-owned nightly units are not lawful. Mid-term furnished rentals to
Swedish and Craig hospital staff are, and are a good business here — see
[the mid-term rental guide](/investing/mid-term-rentals-hospital-corridors).

## Littleton

Littleton adopted an STR licence following its 2022–23 study. The licence
is annual (about $200), and requires: a guest handout of city rules, a local
contact able to respond **within two hours**, occupancy of two per bedroom up
to **eight**, a **28-night maximum stay**, and **one off-street parking space
per bedroom**. Primary-residence requirements apply by zone; confirm the
current text for the parcel. Lodging tax applies.

Downtown Littleton's [historic core](/neighborhoods/littleton/downtown-littleton)
is where the nightly demand is; it is also where the parking standard is
hardest to meet.

## Sheridan

Three square miles, 1950s–60s stock, the metro's cheapest houses south of
Denver. Sheridan licenses STRs through its business licence and lodging tax
with few applicants; it is a long-term yield market. No long-term licence.

## Centennial

No long-term licence. STRs are addressed through the land development code
and business licensing; most of Centennial's housing is in HOAs whose
covenants set minimum lease terms of six or twelve months, which is the
binding constraint. Confirm with the city and pull the covenants.

## Greenwood Village, Cherry Hills Village, Glendale

Greenwood Village and Cherry Hills Village are large-lot, HOA-heavy, and
effectively without STRs; neither licenses long-term rentals. Glendale — the
one-square-mile enclave inside Denver — is overwhelmingly apartments and
regulates STRs through its business licence.

## The south metro fact that matters more

Arapahoe County's mill levies vary by school district: Englewood Schools,
Sheridan Schools, Littleton Public Schools and Cherry Creek Schools each
levy differently, and Cherry Creek's reputation carries a rent premium that
shows up in the tenant pool as much as in the tax bill. See [the Englewood /
Sheridan / Littleton brief](/investing/englewood-sheridan-littleton) and [the
property-tax guide](/investing/property-tax-for-colorado-investors).

## How this changes the underwriting

1. **No long-term licence line** anywhere in the south metro.
2. **Nightly income only where you live** (Englewood) or under a licence
   with real operating constraints (Littleton).
3. **HOA covenants, not city code, govern the east side.** Pull them
   before the HOA deadline.

<Note label="Verify before you write">
  Littleton's and Englewood's ordinances are recent and have been amended.
  Each city publishes an STR FAQ; read the current one before underwriting
  any stay under 30 nights.
</Note>
