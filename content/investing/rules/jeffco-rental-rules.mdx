Jefferson County is where the metro's small landlords live — the 1950s–70s
ranches of Lakewood, Arvada and Wheat Ridge are the most-rented houses in
the state — and it has never licensed long-term rentals. Every jurisdiction
in the county licenses short-term rentals, and no two do it the same way.

## Lakewood

Ordinance O-2023-05 (2023, as amended). An STR is 29 consecutive days or
fewer.

- **Primary-residence STR**: licensed; whole home or part.
- **One additional non-owner-occupied STR** per licensee, added by a 7–4
  council amendment. This is what makes Lakewood the metro's most
  investor-tolerant STR regime.
- Requirements: city STR licence and business licence, a life-safety
  inspection, liability insurance, a local contact, occupancy of two per
  bedroom plus two, lodging tax remittance.
- Annual renewal; licences do not transfer.

Long-term rentals: no licence. Lakewood studied a rental registry in 2024
and did not adopt one.

## Arvada

Business licence plus STR licence for any STR — whole home, rooms, or an ADU.
Arvada's 2026 ordinance update strengthened enforcement, adjusted the
number of STR permits one person may hold, and clarified eligible
licensees. The practical effect is a tighter cap on non-primary units per
owner than before; check the current number on the city's STR page before
you underwrite a second one. Lodging tax applies. Long-term rentals: no
licence.

## Wheat Ridge

STR ordinance effective May 1, 2021; 10% lodgers' tax on every stay.

- **Whole-home (non-owner-occupied) licences** are capped per council
  district at **2% of the one- and two-unit dwellings** in that district,
  set annually. Districts near the 38th Avenue and Ridge Road corridors fill
  first.
- **Partial-home (owner-occupied) licences** are not capped.
- Ownership is required to apply (deed copy on a new purchase); annual
  renewal with current insurance and emergency contact; council has been
  adding fines for operating unlicensed.

Long-term rentals: no licence.

## Golden

Ordinance 2078. In any residential zone (R-1, R-1A, R-2, R-3, RE, residential
PUD) an STR must be the **primary residence of the owner of record**, owner
occupied at least **ten months of the year**. The primary dwelling or an
accessory dwelling may be rented. Every listing must show the city licence
number and the four-adult occupancy statement. Commercial zones are treated
differently. Long-term rentals: no licence; Mines student houses are
regulated only by building code since HB24-1007.

## Edgewater, Morrison, Idaho Springs

Edgewater licenses STRs with a primary-residence requirement. Morrison's
handful of STRs are licensed by the town with a cap. Idaho Springs is in
Clear Creek County, which runs its own STR licence programme with limits on
non-primary units. All three are small enough that you call the clerk.

## Unincorporated Jefferson County — Evergreen, Conifer, Genesee, Indian Hills

Section 46 of the zoning resolution, adopted by the commissioners on
December 16, 2025, replaced the earlier temporary-use approach:

1. **Special exception** from the Board of Adjustment — a public process,
   roughly two months, with neighbour notice.
2. **STR permit** from Planning & Zoning.

Conditions: single-family detached dwelling or portion, no more than five
bedrooms, septic permitted for the occupancy, off-street parking, wildfire
mitigation and defensible space, a local contact, and county lodging tax.
The county treats an STR as a residential use, so HOA covenants in Genesee
and the Evergreen subdivisions can still prohibit it independently.

For [Evergreen](/places/evergreen), the binding constraints are septic and
insurance, not the county. See [the insurance ground notes](/investing/front-range-insurance-hail-wildfire).

## How this changes the underwriting

1. **Long-term is unlicensed countywide.** No licence line, no inspection
   line; state law governs.
2. **Lakewood is the one place in the metro where a second, non-primary STR
   is lawful** for a licensee, and the numbers near Belmar and the W Line
   can work. Wheat Ridge's cap and Arvada's per-person limit make those
   a waiting game.
3. **Foothills STRs are a permitting project**, not a purchase.

<Note label="Verify before you write">
  Arvada changed its rule in 2026 and Jeffco in December 2025. Both
  cities and the county publish the current text; read it the week you
  write the offer.
</Note>
