Everything within ninety minutes of Denver or Colorado Springs that a tourist
would book — Estes Park, Manitou Springs, Nederland, Evergreen, Woodland
Park — has either capped short-term rental licences or built a permitting
process slow enough to act as one. The investor's mistake is to pay a
nightly-rental price for a house whose licence does not exist, does not
transfer, or sits in a zone where it cannot be renewed.

## Estes Park

The gateway to Rocky Mountain National Park and the Front Range's deepest
nightly market.

- **Residential-zone vacation-home licences are capped at 322.** The cap is
  full. The waitlist was cleared in 2025 and the revised regulations
  (effective early 2026) replaced it with a **lottery**, held after annual
  renewals whenever the waitlist falls to ten or fewer applications, to
  refill it to a maximum of 30.
- **Commercial and accommodations zones** are not capped; licences there are
  available but the properties are priced as lodging.
- Requirements: annual licence, life-safety inspection, occupancy of two
  per bedroom plus two (capped at eight in residential zones unless
  grandfathered), a local manager within a set drive time, a wildlife-proof
  trash enclosure, and Estes Park's lodging tax including the 2022 voter-
  approved workforce-housing increment.
- **Transfer:** the 2026 revision sets out when a residential licence
  transfers with a sale. This is the whole question on any Estes purchase;
  get the Town Clerk's written confirmation of licence status, zone and
  transferability as a contract contingency.

A licensed residential vacation home in [Estes Park](/places/estes-park)
trades at a visible premium over an identical unlicensed one, and that
premium is an estimate of the licence's scarcity value. Without the licence,
Estes long-term rents are seasonal and the yield is under 4%.

## Manitou Springs

- STRs capped at **2% of residential structures** (about 56 at the 2013
  baseline). Full.
- **Owner occupancy at least 185 days a year.**
- **500-foot separation** between STRs.
- Annual licence, inspection, lodging tax; the Manitou Avenue commercial
  corridor is treated separately.

Investor-owned nightly rentals in Manitou's residential neighborhoods are
not lawful. [Manitou](/places/manitou-springs) is a primary-residence STR
town and a long-term rental town with a tight tenant pool.

## Nederland

Town STR permits with a cap and a primary/non-primary split, annual renewal,
and Boulder County's wildfire and septic conditions for parcels outside
town limits. Small numbers; check the town's permit page for the current
count and waitlist.

## Evergreen, Conifer, Genesee (unincorporated Jeffco)

Not capped, but permitted through a **special exception** from the Board of
Adjustment plus an STR permit under Section 46 (adopted December 16, 2025):
single-family detached, at most five bedrooms, septic capacity, wildfire
mitigation, parking, local contact. Roughly two months and a public hearing.
HOA covenants in Genesee and most Evergreen subdivisions can prohibit STRs
regardless. See [the Jeffco rules page](/investing/jeffco-rental-rules).

## Woodland Park and Teller County

Woodland Park licenses STRs with a city business licence, occupancy and
parking standards, and lodging tax, without a numeric cap as of August 2026;
Teller County permits STRs in its rural zones through a review process.
[Woodland Park](/places/woodland-park) is the most STR-open of the five, and
the nightly demand is Pikes Peak and Colorado Springs weekenders rather than
a national park.

## Idaho Springs, Georgetown, Lyons, Palmer Lake

Clear Creek County (Idaho Springs, Georgetown) licenses STRs with limits on
non-primary units. Lyons requires a primary residence in most residential
zones. Palmer Lake licenses with a small cap. All four are call-the-clerk
towns.

## How to buy in a capped town

1. **Licence first.** Written confirmation from the town of the licence
   number, zone, status, renewal date and transferability, as a contract
   contingency with a right to terminate.
2. **Underwrite the long-term lease** as the floor. If the long-term number
   does not cover the mortgage with a 40% down payment, the nightly number is
   carrying the whole purchase and a rule change ends it.
3. **Insurance before inspection.** Every one of these towns is in the
   wildland-urban interface; carriers have non-renewed whole zip codes. See
   [the insurance ground notes](/investing/front-range-insurance-hail-wildfire).
4. **Septic and water.** A five-bedroom cabin on a three-bedroom septic
   permit is a three-bedroom rental.

<Note label="Verify before you write">
  Estes Park revised its code in January 2026, Jeffco in December 2025,
  and Nederland and Manitou adjust their caps. The town clerk's answer on
  the day you write the offer is the only one that counts.
</Note>
