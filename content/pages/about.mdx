We answer every question ourselves. We've lived in Baker, Wheat Ridge, and now a
house in Arvada with a bad driveway.

Yes, we sell real estate - it's how this pays for itself - and you'll hear about
it exactly once per page, at the bottom, where it belongs.

## How we decide what to write

Every page on this site started as a question somebody actually asked us. If we
don't know the answer, we go find out, and then it becomes a page. Nothing here
is written to rank for a keyword we have no business ranking for.

## How we handle being wrong

Pages carry a revision date. When something changes - a rule, a price band, a
restaurant that lost its cook - we change the page and move the date. We don't
quietly delete things.
