There is debate. But if you're new and you get one shot, go in this order. Two
of the three are counter-service and one is a window.

## How to order it the first time

Ask for it on the side. Colorado green chile is a sauce, not a soup, and the
best way to judge a kitchen is to put a spoonful next to something plain and see
whether it improves it. If it does, order it on everything for the next year.

## The Pueblo argument

Pueblo chile is a different pepper - the Mirasol - and people from Pueblo will
tell you it is the only real one. They have a point and we are not going to
adjudicate it here.
