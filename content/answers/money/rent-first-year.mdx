Usually yes if you've never spent a winter here, usually no if you already know
which side of the metro you want. The variable isn't the market, it's how
confidently you can name your neighborhood.

## The version almost nobody does

Rent *inside* the town you intend to buy in. Not thirty minutes away, not
"somewhere central." If you think you want Louisville, sign a six-month lease in
Louisville. You will learn in November whether the thing you liked in July was
the town or the weather.

## What a winter actually tests

Light, wind, and the drive. The Front Range gets more sun in January than most
people expect and more wind than anybody expects. The neighborhoods that feel
different in winter are the ones on exposed ground with no mature trees - which
is most of the new construction on the east side.
