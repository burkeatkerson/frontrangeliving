The Front Range's two defining wildfire events were not in the mountains.
Waldo Canyon in June 2012 came over the ridge into Mountain Shadows, a
1980s–90s Colorado Springs subdivision, and burned 346 houses. The Marshall
Fire on December 30, 2021 was a grass fire driven by 100-mph winds across
open space and into Louisville and Superior's 1990s cul-de-sacs; it
destroyed more than 1,000 homes with no forest in sight. If your house
backs onto open space, a greenbelt, a creek corridor or the foothills, this
is your maintenance list too.

## Are you in it

The Colorado State Forest Service's Colorado Wildfire Risk Viewer
(coloradoforestatlas.org) maps risk by address. The practical test:

- West of Highway 93 (Golden–Boulder) or Highway 115 (Springs–Cañon City):
  yes. Evergreen, Genesee, Conifer, the Boulder mountain parcels, Mountain
  Shadows, Peregrine, Rockrimmon, the Broadmoor's west edge, Cheyenne
  Cañon, Manitou, Palmer Lake, Monument's west side, Roxborough, Castle
  Pines, Perry Park.
- On a grassland or open-space edge anywhere: Superior, Louisville, east
  Boulder, Erie, Broomfield's open-space subdivisions, Highlands Ranch's
  backcountry edge, the Bluffs in Lone Tree, Fountain and Security's
  prairie edges, Pueblo West, Black Forest, and any Front Range house
  whose back fence is a greenbelt.
- Anywhere, on a 100-mph downslope wind day with humidity under 10% — the
  Marshall Fire conditions — embers travel a mile.

## Insurance

After Marshall, Cameron Peak and East Troublesome, carriers re-rated the
WUI and left parts of it. In the foothills the mitigation assessment, the
Class A roof and the defensible space are the difference between a
standard-market policy and the FAIR Plan. Document everything with dated
photographs, keep the assessment certificate in the house file, and shop
with an independent agent who places foothills policies routinely. Full
detail in [the insurance guide](/answers/homeowners-insurance-colorado-hail).

## The day it happens

A go-bag, the documents in the cloud, the garage door's manual release
known (power goes first), and a plan for pets. Sign up for the county's
emergency alerts (Everbridge / CodeRED in most Front Range counties; Peak
Alerts in El Paso). When the order comes, leave — the Marshall Fire moved
faster than a car through a neighbourhood, and the houses that survived it
were the ones whose owners had done the list above in the summer, not the
ones whose owners stayed with a hose.
