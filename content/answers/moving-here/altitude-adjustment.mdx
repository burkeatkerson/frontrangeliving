Denver is 5,280 feet, which is high enough to notice and low enough to adapt
to. Expect about two weeks of sleeping badly and feeling slow on stairs, then it
stops being a thing you think about.

## The first week

Drink more water than feels reasonable, ease off alcohol, and do not schedule
your hardest workout for day three. The dry air does as much damage as the thin
air, and most "altitude headaches" in week one are dehydration wearing a costume.

## The mountains are a different question

Going from 5,280 to a trailhead at 10,000 in ninety minutes is a real
elevation change, and it is where people actually get into trouble. Give the
first mountain day a slow pace and a turnaround time.
