Every Front Range parent ends up with a museum rotation, because the
weather here hands you twenty snow days and forty 95°F afternoons a year.
This is the full sort, north to south, with the 2026 prices and the ages
they actually work for — not the ages the brochure claims.

## The metro

| Museum | Ages | 2026 price (adult / child) | The read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Denver Museum of Nature & Science, City Park | 2–14 | $25.95 / $20.95 | The anchor. Dinosaurs, Space Odyssey, the Discovery Zone toddler area, gem mines, mummies. Membership territory. Park in the Colorado Blvd garage; the H2Odyssey splash pad is across the lot for summer exits. |
| Children's Museum of Denver, Marsico Campus | 1–7 | ~$18 flat, under 1 free | The toddler-preschool machine. First Friday evenings run $12 after 4pm some months. In-person tickets only. |
| Denver Art Museum | 5+ | Kids 18 and under free, always | The free-kids policy is the story; the family studios and Ganzeer-style interactive floors are genuinely good. Free days monthly for adults. |
| History Colorado Center | 6–12 | ~$15 / $10 | The Time Machine floor, the ski-jump simulator, Keota's town. Quieter than DMNS on snow days — the insider move. |
| Wings Over the Rockies, Lowry | 4–12 | ~$21 / $13 | A B-52 in a hangar and a Star Wars X-wing. Every plane-phase kid's favourite building. |
| Forney Museum of Transportation | 4–10 | ~$14 / $7 | Locomotives and a Big Boy engine; pairs with a train-phase kid. |

## Boulder County and the north

**WOW! Children's Museum, Lafayette** — $15 for kids 1–11 (adults $9,
which is the right way round), Tuesday–Saturday, discounted after 3pm.
Two floors scaled for under-8s; the Lafayette and Louisville libraries
lend free family passes, which is the local cheat code. **Fort Collins
Museum of Discovery** is the region's real museum — plesiosaur, digital
dome, live animals, music lab — and honours DMNS reciprocal memberships.
**Longmont Museum** has a strong hands-on kids wing and $8 admission.

## The Springs and south

The Springs' paid museum scene is thin, and its free one is excellent:
the **Pioneers Museum** (free, downtown, a real courtroom and a Van
Briggle pottery floor), the two county **nature centers** — [Bear
Creek](/columns/saturday-with-kids/bear-creek-nature-center) and
[Fountain Creek](/columns/saturday-with-kids/fountain-creek-nature-center)
— the **Air Force Academy visitor center** (free, new in 2024, planes and
the cadet chapel overlook), and **Whit's End** at Focus on the Family
(free, a three-storey slide, whatever you make of the sponsor). The
paid oddities are the point of the south: the **May Natural History
Museum** — the "bug museum," 7,000 mounted insects behind a giant
roadside beetle, $6 for kids, May–September — the **Ghost Town Museum**
(~$9), and the **Western Museum of Mining & Industry** (~$11, real steam
machinery running). The **Rocky Mountain Dinosaur Resource Center** in
Woodland Park (~$13 kids) is covered in [the dinosaur guide](/answers/dinosaurs-with-kids-colorado).

**Pueblo:** the **Buell Children's Museum** (~$10, attached to the Sangre
de Cristo Arts Center) is the most under-rated kids museum in the state,
and the [City Park rides](/columns/saturday-with-kids/pueblo-city-park-rides)
next to the zoo are 25 cents.

## How to not pay full price

Almost everything above is in the SCFD (metro) or has a library pass or
free day — the [free-days strategy](/answers/scfd-free-days-memberships)
is the companion piece. The short version: join the one your kids ask
for twice (DMNS south of Longmont, FCMOD north of it), use free days and
library passes for the rest, and never pay walk-up price at the
Aquarium.

## The snow-day dispatch order

Storm forecast tonight? DMNS and the Children's Museum will be at
capacity by 10:30. Go at opening, or go contrarian: History Colorado,
Wings Over the Rockies and the Forney absorb a snow-day crowd without
feeling like one, and the [rec centers](/answers/rec-centers-swim-lessons-kids)
are open too. The full bad-weather list is in [the rainy-day guide](/answers/rainy-day-indoor-kids-front-range).
