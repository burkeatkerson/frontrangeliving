## The gaps nobody warns you about

**The hours gap:** museum camps run 9–4 against a 9–5 job; aftercare
sells separately and sells out. **The last two weeks of July / first
of August** are the thinnest catalog weeks — book those first, not
last. **Jump weeks** (the district's session break) differ by
provider; two providers' schedules rarely align, which is why the
same-provider-multiple-weeks move wins. And **half-day preschool
camps** are recreation, not childcare — the math only works stacked
with a nanny-share or a flexible schedule.

## The free-range alternative

A parent home for the summer replaces the machine with the [free
list](/answers/free-things-kids-front-range) run as a rotation: the
pool pass, library summer-reading (with prizes), the splash pads,
one [camping night](/answers/first-camping-trip-kids), the June
free fishing weekend, and a weekly "camp day" with the neighbors.
The eleven weeks are long; that is their whole charm.
