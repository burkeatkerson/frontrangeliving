The metro has a lot of neighborhoods that look walkable and are cut in half by
a six-lane road. These three are not. We've ridden all of them with a
nine-year-old.

## The arterial test

Pull up the route from the front door to the school. Count how many times it
crosses a road with four or more lanes and no signal. If the answer is anything
above zero, the kid will ride it twice and then ask for a ride. This is the
single variable that separates neighborhoods that *look* bikeable from ones
where you actually see kids on bikes at 3pm.

## Winter is the real filter

Fort Collins is on this list because kids ride there in February. That only
happens where the city plows bike infrastructure on the same schedule as the
roads, which is a budget decision, not a geography one.
