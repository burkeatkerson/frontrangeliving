The Front Range privatized almost nothing and district-ized almost
everything: recreation here mostly belongs to special districts with
tax bases, which is why the pools are this good and why the rules
change every few miles. Learn your district, its registration dates,
and the MY Denver card if you're in the city, and the whole system
opens up.

## Swim lessons: the actual pipeline

The standard Front Range swim path: **parent-tot** (6mo–3yr,
water-comfort), **preschool levels** (3–5), **learn-to-swim levels
1–6** (5+), then swim team or done. District group lessons ($60–110
per 6–8 session block) follow the same Red Cross-derived levels
everywhere, so progress transfers between districts. The
alternatives: **summer intensives** (daily for two weeks — the
fastest route to water-safe), **private lessons** ($40–90/half hour
at swim schools like SafeSplash, Big Blue, Goldfish — faster for the
fearful kid, spendy for the fine one), and the **YMCA's** strong
scholarship-backed program in the Springs. Register the opening
morning; take the off-peak slot; and treat "water-safe by 6" as the
deadline, because this is a state of unfenced reservoirs and
[fast creeks](/answers/swim-beaches-lakes-kids).

## What else the card gets you

The rec center is the metro's cheap everything-else: **open gym**
and **tot time** ($3–6), **drop-in youth basketball/volleyball**,
**climbing walls** with staff hours (Carla Madison, Apex, EPIC),
**ice skating** at the district rinks ($8–12 with skates), **youth
sports leagues** ($60–150 a season — the feeder for everything, and
registration windows mirror swim lessons), and **no-school-day
camps** that solve the random Friday off. Denver's card adds free
after-school programs; every district's scholarship fund (never
advertised, always real) cuts fees 25–100% for qualifying families.

## Which ones justify a drive

For a leisure-pool day: **Apex** (Arvada), **Paul Derda**
(Broomfield), **Westminster City Park**, **EPIC** (Fort Collins),
**Carla Madison** (Denver, with the rooftop). For ice: **Apex** and
**South Suburban's** arenas, **Sertich** in the Springs. For the
full-family Saturday: Denver's **Central Park** center next to its
namesake [playground](/answers/destination-playgrounds-front-range).
The [water-park tier list](/answers/water-parks-front-range) picks
up where the leisure pools top out, and the [winter rescue
list](/answers/rainy-day-indoor-kids-front-range) is built on
exactly these buildings.
