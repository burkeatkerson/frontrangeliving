Colorado's two big zoos are 70 miles and a whole philosophy apart. Denver
is a classic city zoo — flat, broad, encyclopedic. Cheyenne Mountain is a
mountainside institution that decided to do fewer things at eye level
with the animals, and keeps getting ranked among the best zoos in the
country for it. Families in the middle — Douglas County, Castle Rock,
Monument — genuinely have to choose. Here is the choice.

## The age flip

The toddler zoo trip is a stroller loop with three animal stops and a
snack, and Denver is built for exactly that: flat paths, shade, the
carousel, a dozen exits to the car. Cheyenne Mountain with a 2-year-old
is a workout with a view.

Around age five it flips. The CMZoo giraffe deck, the wallaby walkabout,
the rocky-mountain-goat cliffs and the Sky Ride are an adventure in a way
a flat zoo cannot be, and a seven-year-old will walk the grade without
noticing. Denver's counterpunch for the older kid is depth — Tropical
Discovery is the best reptile house between the coasts — but the
memorable day tilts south.

## Doing each one right

**Denver:** book the timed ticket for opening, do elephants and
Tropical Discovery before 11, picnic on the City Park lawn outside the
gate (re-entry with a wristband), and pair the afternoon with the
[DMNS](/answers/museums-with-kids-front-range) across the park or the
H2Odyssey splash pad in summer. Park free along the City Park loop or
in the museum lot. Zoo Lights in December ($19–26) is a separate,
excellent, cold evening — see [holiday lights](/answers/holiday-lights-with-kids).

**Cheyenne Mountain:** buy the timed ticket for the first morning slot
(cheaper and cooler), drive up for the 11am giraffe window, work
downhill, and take the Sky Ride when legs quit. The zoo ticket includes
the Shrine of the Sun drive — the tower on the mountain above, five
minutes up, with the whole Springs below it. Electric Safari in December
is the Springs' Zoo Lights. Pair the morning with [Bear Creek Nature
Center](/columns/saturday-with-kids/bear-creek-nature-center) or the
[Broadmoor's free lake loop](/places/colorado-springs) below.

## The third answer

The **Pueblo Zoo** is what a zoo was in 1985, in the good sense: $12ish
admission, two shaded hours, penguins, lions, a walkable loop, and the
[25-cent City Park rides](/columns/saturday-with-kids/pueblo-city-park-rides)
next door. For the under-5 household south of Castle Rock it is the
most proportionate zoo day on the Front Range. The **Denver Aquarium**
and the **Butterfly Pavilion** round out the animal circuit — both in
[the museum guide](/answers/museums-with-kids-front-range) — and the
free wildlife option is the drive through **Rocky Mountain Arsenal
National Wildlife Refuge**, where the bison herd is fifteen minutes
from downtown and costs nothing.
