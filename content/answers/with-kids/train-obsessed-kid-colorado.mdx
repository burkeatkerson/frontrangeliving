## The Golden Saturday

The default train Saturday is Golden: the [Railroad Museum](/columns/saturday-with-kids/colorado-railroad-museum)
at opening (two hours: roundhouse, turntable, climb the cabooses, run
the garden railway buttons), lunch in [downtown Golden](/neighborhoods/golden/downtown-golden),
and the W Line home if you are feeling thematic. Steam-up and
operating days are listed on the museum's calendar — a ride day is
worth planning around, and the December Polar Express sells out by
October.

## The event trains

Colorado's tourist lines run the kid calendar hard: **Day Out With
Thomas** at the Railroad Museum (spring), **pumpkin and Santa trains**
on the Georgetown Loop, the Royal Gorge's **Santa Express**, and the
cog's early-winter Christmas runs. They cost more, book out weeks
ahead, and land best at ages 2–6. The regular trains are better trains;
the event trains are better birthdays.

## Altitude, storms, and other fine print

The Georgetown Loop sits at 8,500 feet and the cog tops out at 14,115:
water, sun, layers, and watch kids for altitude headaches — descending
fixes it. Mountain lines run rain-or-shine but afternoon lightning can
hold the cog's summit time; book mornings. Strollers ride the Georgetown
and Royal Gorge trains fine; the cog wants a carrier. And every one of
these lines is cheaper in the shoulder months — a September Wednesday on
the Loop with the aspens turning is the best train day of the year, and
[the fall guide](/answers/fall-with-kids-front-range) builds the rest of
that day around it.
