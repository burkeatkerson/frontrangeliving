version of this place is free — the geology, the creeks, the civic
layer, the parade calendar — and that the paid attractions are the
garnish. This is the master list, organized as a rotation, because
"what's free" is really "what's this weekend."

## The scheduled free layer

The [SCFD free days and zoo lottery](/answers/scfd-free-days-memberships)
carry the museums; the rest of the calendar:

- **Free fishing weekend** — June 6–7, 2026 — plus [kids always free](/answers/fishing-with-kids-colorado).
- **Levitt Pavilion Denver**: ~50 free concerts at Ruby Hill all
  summer; blankets, kids dancing, $0.
- **City Park Jazz** (Denver, Sunday evenings, June–July) and every
  suburb's free summer concert series.
- **Parade season**: St. Patrick's (March), Cinco de Mayo, the
  Greeley Stampede's 4th of July, your town's homecoming, the 9News
  Parade of Lights, and January's **stock show kickoff parade** —
  longhorns down 17th Street, free from any curb.
- **National Western Stock Show grounds days** (January): the free
  and cheap layers — the hill of trade barns, the petting corrals on
  value days — around the ticketed rodeos.
- **Open streets days**: Denver's ¡Viva! Streets and Boulder's
  open-Pearl Sundays hand kids a car-free arterial to bike.
- **First Fridays**: the Santa Fe and Tennyson art walks are
  stroller-friendly before 7pm, and the galleries hand out cookies.
- **Library summer reading** (June–July): every district, with
  actual prize books and free-ticket coupons — the quiet engine of
  the whole summer.

## The 52-weekend engine

The rotation that never repeats: one **creek or lake** weekend
([splash pads](/answers/splash-pads-front-range) → [beaches](/answers/swim-beaches-lakes-kids)
in summer, [sledding](/answers/sledding-hills-front-range) in
winter), one **trail** weekend ([the kid hikes](/answers/hikes-kids-actually-finish)),
one **civic** weekend (library event, free day, parade, tour), and
one **wildcard** ([bike park](/answers/biking-with-kids-front-range),
[pond fishing](/answers/fishing-with-kids-colorado), the bison
drive, a [free-tier farm visit](/answers/fall-with-kids-front-range)
in season). Paid things — [the zoo](/answers/which-zoo-denver-or-cheyenne-mountain),
[Water World](/answers/water-parks-front-range), [Casa Bonita](/answers/casa-bonita-meow-wolf-worth-it)
— slot in monthly-ish, which is exactly often enough to stay
special.

The deep truth of the list: at ages 2 through 10, the kids cannot
tell the difference between the $200 Saturday and the $0 one with a
creek in it. The creek one usually wins.
