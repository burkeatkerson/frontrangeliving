morning and the 40-mph-wind afternoon — and both produce the same
problem: every parent in the metro has the same six ideas between 10:30
and 2. The list below is sorted by the thing that actually matters,
which is when each option fills.

## Under 5: the morning circuit

The winning toddler weather day is a circuit, not a destination:
**storytime at 10** (free, every district — Denver, Arapahoe, Jeffco,
Douglas, Anythink, Boulder, PPLD — most branches run babies, toddlers
and preschool sessions weekly), **lunch somewhere with a play area**,
**nap**, and if the afternoon still needs killing, **tot open-gym at the
rec center** — $3–6 for a gym full of mats, trikes and parachute games,
listed as "tot time" or "open gym" on every district's schedule.

The [Children's Museum](/answers/museums-with-kids-front-range) and WOW
are the paid upgrades, at opening only. The Butterfly Pavilion is 75
minutes of warm humidity in January, which is its own argument.

## Ages 6–12: burn the energy first

Order of operations on a cooped-up Saturday: something physical first —
open swim, open jump, a climbing gym, open gym at the rec center — then
the museum, then the couch. Doing the museum first produces the gift-shop
meltdown; every parent learns this once.

The under-used physical options: **rec-center open gym** (basketballs
and dodgeballs, $3–6), **drop-in skating** at the district ice rinks
(Apex, South Suburban, Sertich in the Springs — $8–12 with skates),
**roller rinks** (Skate City's three Springs locations and Wagon Wheel
in Brighton are time capsules in the best way), and **open climb**
hours where staff run the ropes so you do not have to learn to belay.

## The Springs' own list

Whit's End (free), the [Penny Arcade in Manitou](/columns/saturday-with-kids/manitou-penny-arcade)
(bring a bag of quarters — most of it still costs under a dollar),
the two nature centers' indoor exhibit rooms, the Pioneers Museum
(free), iFLY indoor skydiving (splurge tier), Great Wolf's day pass,
and the YMCA's seven-plus locations, whose $10 youth day pass includes
the pools. The [Springs museum roundup](/answers/museums-with-kids-front-range)
has the rest.

## Two rules the locals follow

**The 10:30 rule.** Everything famous is full from 10:30 to 2 on a
weather day. Opening bell or 2:30 entry; nothing in between.

**The sun comes back.** The Front Range storm that dumps a foot at 8am
is regularly 45°F and blinding by 2 — at which point the answer is not
indoors at all, it is [the sledding hill](/answers/sledding-hills-front-range)
while the snow is still good, because by tomorrow it will be mud.
