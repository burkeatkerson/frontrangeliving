Colorado sells itself with gold-medal rivers, but the fishery that
matters to a seven-year-old is a park pond four minutes from the
house, stocked by a state truck with rainbows that bite worms. The
state built the whole system to be free for kids and nearly effortless
for parents. Here is how to plug into it.

## The rules, in one breath

15 and under: free, always, no paperwork. Adults: about $12 a day or
low-$40s a year (buy in the CPW app in the parking lot), free
statewide on **June 6–7, 2026**. Bag limits at most stocked ponds are
four trout; some kids-only ponds and special regs are posted at the
water. That is the entire legal load.

## Where the fish are, by home base

| Area | Ponds that produce | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Denver | Berkeley Lake, Rocky Mountain Lake, Garfield Lake, Huston Lake, Sloan's Lake (bigger water) | All city parks; stocked spring–fall; bathrooms at the rec centers |
| Arvada / west | Lake Arbor, Kendrick Lake (Lakewood), Main Reservoir, Prospect Park ponds (Wheat Ridge along Clear Creek) | Kendrick is the west side's ringer |
| North metro | Standley Lake shoreline, Ketner Lake, Hidden Lake, Brighton's Ken Mitchell ponds | Adams County ponds get bass and bluegill too |
| Boulder County | Waneka Lake (Lafayette), Thomas Reservoir (Erie), Izaak Walton ponds (Longmont), Golden Ponds | Waneka has a boathouse and path |
| South metro | Ketring Lake (Littleton), Johnson's Habitat (Denver's kid-priority pond on the Platte), Quincy Reservoir (bigger), deKoevend ponds | Johnson's Habitat is designed for exactly this |
| Douglas County | Bingham Lake (Castle Rock), Salisbury ponds (Parker) | Stocked spring |
| Fort Collins / north | Sheldon Lake (City Park), Riverbend Ponds, Flatiron Reservoir (Loveland — a stocking magnet), Boyd Lake | Flatiron is worth the drive |
| Colorado Springs | Prospect Lake, Quail Lake, Palmer Lake, the nature-center ponds at Fountain Creek | Prospect gets heavy urban stockings |
| Pueblo | Runyon Lake and ponds, Lake Pueblo (big water) | Runyon is the south's Johnson's Habitat |

The mountain upgrade once the pond works: **Evergreen Lake**, **Bear
Creek Reservoir**, **Georgetown's silver ponds**, and **Eleven Mile /
Spinney** for the family that gets serious. Moving water with kids
starts at **Clear Creek in Golden** and **the Poudre's town stretch**
— but ponds first; rivers eat tackle and patience.

## The clinic shortcut

You do not have to be the expert. CPW and the cities run free
learn-to-fish events all summer — rods, bait and rangers provided:
CPW's family fishing clinics at the state parks (calendar on the CPW
site, clustered around the June free weekend), Colorado Springs'
Get Outdoors Day at Memorial Park (free kid rods while they last),
Denver's Fishing Derby days at city lakes, and most rec districts'
one-morning derbies. A kid who catches three fish at a clinic is a
kid who asks to go again; that is the whole product.
