Front Range fall is short, spectacular and double-booked: the aspen
turn and the farm season overlap in a six-week window that also
contains the first snow. The families who get it all in run it as a
schedule, not a mood. Here is the schedule.

## The aspen day, kid version

The gold drive works with kids when it contains a walk under two
miles and a treat town. The four proven combinations:

- **Kenosha Pass** (US-285): the Colorado Trail ambles through the
  metro's most famous groves within half a mile of the lot —
  arrive before 9 on the peak weekends, truly — then pie in Bailey.
- **Guanella Pass** from Georgetown: golden switchbacks, tundra at
  the top, and the [Georgetown Loop's pumpkin trains](/answers/train-obsessed-kid-colorado)
  running below.
- **Golden Gate Canyon State Park** ($10/car): the Aspen Meadow
  loops are the closest big groves to Denver, with picnic tables in
  them.
- **From the Springs:** Mueller State Park plus the Crags road, or
  Gold Camp Road's shelf above the city — with doughnuts in
  Manitou after.

## The farm tier list

| Farm | Where | The read | Cost reality |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Anderson Farms | Erie | The big one: mega-maze, wagon rides, everything | ~$25–30 wknd admission; $10 pumpkins, card only |

| Rock Creek Farm | Broomfield | Pick from the actual vine; low-frills, U-cut | Cheap; pay for pumpkins by weight |
| Miller Farms | Platteville | The harvest ride — kids dig their own vegetables | ~$20–25; you leave with produce |
| Cottonwood Farm | Boulder County | Small, sweet, toddler-right | Modest |
| Mazzotti / Fritzler Farm Park | Greeley / LaSalle | NoCo's big festival + the famous night maze | Festival pricing; Fritzler's Scream Acres for teens |
| Diana's Pumpkin Patch & Corn Maze | Cañon City | The south's family classic | Modest |
| Wishing Star / Venetucci tradition | Springs area | The Springs' patch scene, reborn | Varies |
| Happy Apple Farm | Penrose | Actual apple U-pick + pumpkins | Pay by the pound; go early for apples |

One big farm, once, is the season. The second pumpkin trip goes to
the cheap tier or the grocery store, and nobody remembers the
difference by November.

## Halloween infrastructure

The costume-wearing events that don't require a plan: **Zoo Boo** at
Cheyenne Mountain and Denver's **Boo at the Zoo** (trick-or-treat
stations, ticketed, sell out), the **Botanic Gardens'** Glow events,
merchant trick-or-treats in every old downtown (Olde Town Arvada,
Old Colorado City, Downtown Littleton, Pearl Street — the Thursday
or Saturday before Halloween, check the district calendars), and
Pueblo's Boo at the Zoo. The teen tier — Fritzler's Scream Acres,
the 13th Floor, haunted corn at Anderson's — runs the same weekends;
read age recommendations honestly.

The first snow usually lands mid-October and melts by noon two days
later — [sledding rules](/answers/sledding-hills-front-range) apply
for exactly those mornings, and the winter calendar picks up at
[the holiday lights](/answers/holiday-lights-with-kids).
