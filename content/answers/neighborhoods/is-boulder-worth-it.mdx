Honest answer: for about a third of the people who ask, yes, and for the rest
there is a town twenty minutes away that gives them 85% of it for two-thirds of
the money. Here's how we'd triage it.

## The one question that decides it

Will you walk to a trailhead? Not drive - walk, from your door, on a weekday,
more than once a week. If the honest answer is yes, Boulder proper is the only
version of this that works, and the premium is what it costs. If the honest
answer is "we'd drive on weekends," you are paying $300K for a view you could
have from Louisville.

## What you actually give up outside the city limits

Not much, and not what people expect. You keep the schools, you keep the
Flatirons on the windshield, you keep the bike infrastructure. You lose the
walk-out-the-door trail access and you lose being *in* Boulder on a Thursday
night without planning it.
