This is the most common question we get, and it has a good answer, because the
west side of the metro was built out of exactly these three things. Anything
below is a place we'd send a friend without a caveat.

## Why the west side and not the east

The east metro grew after 1995, which means bigger houses on smaller lots, more
HOAs, and food that clusters into shopping centers rather than onto streets. The
west side grew between 1948 and 1972, when the lot was the point. You get a
quarter acre, an alley, a detached garage someone has already wired, and an
arterial - Federal, Sheridan, Kipling, Alameda - that has been feeding people
for fifty years.

## What "yard" actually means here

A Front Range yard is not an East Coast yard. Assume no mature shade unless
somebody planted it, assume you are watering, and assume hail once every three
or four summers. Ask about the sprinkler system before you ask about the kitchen.

## The commute caveat nobody says out loud

Every number above is a *car* number to downtown Denver between 7:30 and 9:00.
If you are commuting to Boulder, only Westminster works. If you are commuting to
the Tech Center, none of these three do, and we'd point you south instead.
