Fort Logan is a south-west Denver neighborhood built around an 1887 army post,
part of which is now a national cemetery. It is quiet, historically distinctive,
and among the more affordable corners of the city.

## Where Fort Logan actually is

South-west Denver between Sheridan and Federal, south of Hampden toward the city
limits, adjoining Sheridan and Littleton.

## The post

Fort Logan was established in 1887 and operated as an army post for decades.
Today the site contains **Fort Logan National Cemetery**, a state mental health
campus, and a number of preserved historic buildings including the officers'
quarters.

## The housing

Mixed 1950s–80s houses and townhomes, generally modest and priced below the
Denver median.

## Who Fort Logan suits

Value buyers who want a quiet corner of Denver with genuine history and easy
foothills access.

## What to check before you offer

**Institutional adjacency** — the cemetery and mental health campus are
significant land uses.

**Mid-century systems.**

Prices here move with the wider market rather than independently of it. The
Denver metro single-family median was $650,000 in June 2026, with condos and
townhomes at $395,000 — useful as a baseline, but a neighborhood this specific
deserves a look at genuinely comparable sales rather than a city average. The
[property tax](https://dpt.colorado.gov/residential-local-government-assessment-rate) picture follows the same statewide formula
everywhere: assessment rate against actual value, multiplied by the local mill
levy.
