Harvey Park South is the quieter continuation of Harvey Park — the same 1950s
and 60s brick ranches without the lake or the architectural pedigree, at a
slightly lower number.

## Where it actually is

South-west Denver between Sheridan and Federal, from Jewell down to Yale, south
of [Harvey Park](/neighborhoods/denver/harvey-park/).

## The housing

1950s–60s brick ranches on standard lots, largely owner-occupied and generally
well maintained.

## Who Harvey Park South suits

First-time buyers and families who want a detached house with a yard below the
Denver median.

## What to check before you offer

**Sewer scope** and **electrical capacity**.

**Roof age.**

Prices here move with the wider market rather than independently of it. The
Denver metro single-family median was $650,000 in June 2026, with condos and
townhomes at $395,000 — useful as a baseline, but a neighborhood this specific
deserves a look at genuinely comparable sales rather than a city average. The
[property tax](https://dpt.colorado.gov/residential-local-government-assessment-rate) picture follows the same statewide formula
everywhere: assessment rate against actual value, multiplied by the local mill
levy.
