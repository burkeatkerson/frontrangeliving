Hampden South is the last strip of Denver before the county line, dense with
apartments and condos, and about ten minutes from the Tech Center. It is a
commuter neighborhood and makes no pretence otherwise.

## Where it actually is

The southern edge of Denver between Colorado Boulevard and Quebec, south of
Hampden Avenue to the city limits, adjoining
[Greenwood Village](/places/greenwood-village/).

## The housing

Predominantly 1970s–90s apartments, condos and townhomes. Detached single-family
inventory is limited.

## Transit and commuting

**Southmoor station** on the light rail gives a direct ride to the Tech Center
and downtown, and I-25 access is immediate.

## Who Hampden South suits

Tech Center workers, renters, and condo buyers who want a Denver address at
south-suburban convenience.

## What to check before you offer

**HOA health** on 1970s and 80s buildings.

**I-25 and Hampden noise.**

**Rental licence status** if buying an existing rental.

Prices here move with the wider market rather than independently of it. The
Denver metro single-family median was $650,000 in June 2026, with condos and
townhomes at $395,000 — useful as a baseline, but a neighborhood this specific
deserves a look at genuinely comparable sales rather than a city average. The
[property tax](https://dpt.colorado.gov/residential-local-government-assessment-rate) picture follows the same statewide formula
everywhere: assessment rate against actual value, multiplied by the local mill
levy.
