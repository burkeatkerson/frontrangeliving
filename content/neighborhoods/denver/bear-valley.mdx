Bear Valley sits at Denver's south-west corner, closer to the foothills than
almost anywhere else in the city, with mid-century houses and prices below the
median.

## Where Bear Valley actually is

South-west Denver between Sheridan and Federal, from Jewell down to Hampden,
adjoining [Lakewood](/places/lakewood/).

## The foothills advantage

Bear Creek Canyon and [Morrison](/places/morrison/) are about fifteen minutes
west, and the Bear Creek Trail runs through the neighborhood. For anyone hiking
or climbing regularly, that access is the reason to be here.

## The housing

1960s–70s ranches and split-levels on suburban lots, plus some condo and
townhome development.

## Who Bear Valley suits

Buyers who head to the mountains often, and value buyers who want a detached
mid-century house with a yard.

## What to check before you offer

**Flood mapping** near Bear Creek.

**1970s systems** and original windows.

Prices here move with the wider market rather than independently of it. The
Denver metro single-family median was $650,000 in June 2026, with condos and
townhomes at $395,000 — useful as a baseline, but a neighborhood this specific
deserves a look at genuinely comparable sales rather than a city average. The
[property tax](https://dpt.colorado.gov/residential-local-government-assessment-rate) picture follows the same statewide formula
everywhere: assessment rate against actual value, multiplied by the local mill
levy.
