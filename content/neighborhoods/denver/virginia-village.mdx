Virginia Village is several thousand mid-century brick ranches in south-east
Denver, built fast in the 1950s and largely intact since. It has become a
destination for buyers who want mid-century houses and a price below the city
median.

## Where Virginia Village actually is

South-east Denver between Colorado Boulevard and Holly, from Mississippi to
Evans, north of [University Hills](/neighborhoods/denver/university-hills/).

## The housing

Overwhelmingly 1950s: brick ranches on standard lots with attached or detached
garages. Within that, there are genuine mid-century modern pockets that trade at
a premium to the standard stock.

## The parks

**Cook Park** has a recreation centre and pool; **Garland Park** and the Cherry
Creek Trail run along the northern side, giving a car-free route into downtown
and out toward Aurora.

## Who Virginia Village suits

Buyers who want a detached house with a yard below the Denver median, and
mid-century enthusiasts.

## What to check before you offer

**Sewer scope** and **electrical panel capacity** — 1950s service is often
inadequate for modern loads.

**Original windows and insulation**, which are common and costly to address.

Prices here move with the wider market rather than independently of it. The
Denver metro single-family median was $650,000 in June 2026, with condos and
townhomes at $395,000 — useful as a baseline, but a neighborhood this specific
deserves a look at genuinely comparable sales rather than a city average. The
[property tax](https://dpt.colorado.gov/residential-local-government-assessment-rate) picture follows the same statewide formula
everywhere: assessment rate against actual value, multiplied by the local mill
levy.
