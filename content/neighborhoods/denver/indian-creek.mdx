Indian Creek is a small, mostly multifamily pocket in south-east Denver, close
to the Cherry Creek corridor and priced well below the city average.

## Where Indian Creek actually is

South-east Denver between Holly and Quebec, from Evans down to Yale, near
[Bible Park](/neighborhoods/denver/hampden/) and the Cherry Creek Trail.

## The housing

Predominantly 1960s–80s apartments and townhomes. Detached single-family
inventory is limited.

## Who Indian Creek suits

Renters and investors looking for below-average pricing inside Denver with
trail and park access.

## What to check before you offer

**HOA health** on older condo and townhome buildings.

**Building systems** on 1970s multifamily stock.

Prices here move with the wider market rather than independently of it. The
Denver metro single-family median was $650,000 in June 2026, with condos and
townhomes at $395,000 — useful as a baseline, but a neighborhood this specific
deserves a look at genuinely comparable sales rather than a city average. The
[property tax](https://dpt.colorado.gov/residential-local-government-assessment-rate) picture follows the same statewide formula
everywhere: assessment rate against actual value, multiplied by the local mill
levy.
