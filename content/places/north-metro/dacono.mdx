Dacono is the third and smallest Carbon Valley town, closest to I-25 and
cheapest of the three. It has less of everything than Firestone and Frederick,
including price.

## Where Dacono actually is

Right on I-25 in southern Weld County, immediately south of
[Firestone](/places/firestone/). Denver is about thirty-three minutes — the
shortest Denver commute of the three Carbon Valley towns.

## Who Dacono is right for

Price-first buyers who want St. Vrain schools and I-25 access. Amenity is
minimal; most errands happen in Firestone, Frederick or Longmont.

## The money question

Below the Weld County median of $499,000 for comparable stock. This is the
value entry point for the northern I-25 corridor with St. Vrain schools.

Property tax here follows the same statewide formula as everywhere else in
Colorado: the residential assessment rate is applied to actual value, then
multiplied by the local mill levy, which is set by the county, the
municipality, the school district, and any special districts stacked on top.
Two identical houses on opposite sides of a district line can carry
meaningfully different bills, so check the levy for the specific parcel rather
than the city average. See the [Division of Property
Taxation](https://dpt.colorado.gov/residential-local-government-assessment-rate) for the current rate.
