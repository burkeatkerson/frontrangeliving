Louisville is what people mean when they say they want Boulder but cannot pay
for Boulder. It is an old coal town with a real main street, and the bike
network goes *under* the busy roads rather than across them.

## Why the underpasses matter

It is the reason children ride bikes here. The town made a deliberate,
expensive infrastructure choice decades ago and it is now the most valuable
thing about living in it.
