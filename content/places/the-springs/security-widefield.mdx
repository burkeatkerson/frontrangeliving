function as one place of nearly forty thousand people, built largely around Fort
Carson employment, and among the least expensive housing in the region.

## Where Security-Widefield actually is

Between Colorado Springs and Fountain along US-85 and I-25, fifteen minutes from
central Colorado Springs.

## Who it is right for

Military families and value buyers, and investors looking for the strongest
rent-to-price ratios in the region. It is a weaker fit for anyone prioritising
schools or amenity over price.

## The money question

Among the lowest prices in the Pikes Peak region, well under the Colorado
Springs median of $499,900. Strong rental demand from the military population.
Do the PFAS due diligence with the specific water district.

Property tax here follows the same statewide formula as everywhere else in
Colorado: the residential assessment rate is applied to actual value, then
multiplied by the local mill levy, which is set by the county, the
municipality, the school district, and any special districts stacked on top.
Two identical houses on opposite sides of a district line can carry
meaningfully different bills, so check the levy for the specific parcel rather
than the city average. See the [Division of Property
Taxation](https://dpt.colorado.gov/residential-local-government-assessment-rate) for the current rate.
