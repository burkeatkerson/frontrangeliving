Athmar Park is the answer for people who want a Denver address, a yard, and a
number that starts with a four. It is not pretty from the arterial and it is
very pleasant six houses in.

## The trail

The South Platte trail runs along the eastern edge. From most of the
neighborhood you are on car-free pavement within four minutes and downtown
within twenty on a bike.
