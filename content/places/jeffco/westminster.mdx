Westminster is the city people move to when they need to be able to reach both
Denver and Boulder without committing to either. That sounds like a compromise
and mostly is not.

## The tax quirk

The city sits across two counties. Two identical houses four blocks apart can
carry meaningfully different property tax bills. Check the county before you
check the comps.
