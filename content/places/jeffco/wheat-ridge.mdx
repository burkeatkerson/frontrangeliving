Wheat Ridge was farmland that became a suburb without ever fully deciding to,
which is why the lots are enormous and the streets have alleys. It is the
answer to more of our questions than any other single city on the Range.

## What you're buying

A 1954 brick ranch on 9,000 square feet with a detached garage somebody already
wired for a shop. Roughly a third of them have had the kitchen done and two
thirds have not. The ones that have not are the deal.

## What to check

Sewer scope, always - the clay lines from this era are at the end of their
lives. Hail history on the roof. And whether the "finished basement" was
permitted, because a lot of them were not.
